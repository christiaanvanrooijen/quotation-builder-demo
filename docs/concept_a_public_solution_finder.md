# Concept A — Public Solution Finder

A public-facing digital brochure or website funnel.

## Goal

Guide prospects from a business challenge to the most relevant PFM insight direction.

## Output

Structured lead payload for n8n and Odoo.

## Best for

- Website lead generation
- Trade fair follow-up
- Commercial education
- Helping prospects understand KPI value before talking to sales

## Human review

Sales should review the lead before any commercial quote is sent.
