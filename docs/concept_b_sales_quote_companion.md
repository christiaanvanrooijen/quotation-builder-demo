# Concept B — Sales Quote Companion

An internal sales enablement tool.

## Goal

Help sales turn a meeting, ROI calculator discussion or customer request into structured quote input.

## Output

Structured quote payload for n8n and Odoo.

## Best for

- Shortening the quote preparation process
- Standardising input across countries
- Supporting new sales colleagues
- Creating draft quotes or indicative proposals

## Human review

Always required before sending anything to the customer.
