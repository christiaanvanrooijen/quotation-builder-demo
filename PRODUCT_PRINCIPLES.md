# Product Principles

## Purpose

The Digital Quote Builder is not a quotation tool.

It is a guided sales conversation that ends with a quotation.

---

## Primary Goal

Help a salesperson have a better conversation.

Generating the quote is only the outcome.

---

## Principles

- Conversation before configuration
- Business value before pricing
- ROI before hardware
- Human review before automation
- Odoo is the commercial source of truth
- AI supports the salesperson; it never replaces commercial judgement
- Every screen should help move the customer to the next conversation
- Less clicking, less scrolling, less explaining

---

## Design Principles

- One clear action per screen
- Progressive disclosure
- Mobile friendly
- Commercial language
- Trustworthy calculations
- Explain assumptions
